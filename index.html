01 — About

Turning ideas into software that people actually use.

I'm a software engineer with an academic background in Computer Science and Engineering (M.Sc.). I like the part of the job where a vague problem becomes a clear data model, then a working feature, then something a real person can rely on.

Most of my work sits around Python — FastAPI, Flask, Django, and Streamlit for data-heavy tooling — alongside PHP/Laravel and front-ends in React, Tailwind, and plain JavaScript. I have also worked with relational databases across PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, SQL Server, and Oracle.

I'm currently an Executive at AristoPharma Ltd. in Dhaka, and I keep learning in public: building side projects, writing documentation, and sharing what I learn on YouTube.

  • Build something real, then improve it in public.
  • Learn the fundamentals instead of memorising the framework.
  • Share what works — and what broke on the way there.
